BANKING CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

1. What is a savings account?
A savings account is a bank account that allows customers to deposit money and earn interest on their savings.

2. What is the minimum balance?
The minimum balance requirement depends on the bank and the type of savings account. Some accounts may have zero minimum balance requirements.

3. What is a current account?
A current account is generally designed for businesses and customers who make frequent transactions.

4. What is a personal loan?
A personal loan is an unsecured loan offered by a bank based on the customer's eligibility and repayment capacity.

5. What is a home loan?
A home loan is a secured loan provided by a bank to purchase, construct, or renovate a residential property.

6. What is a credit card?
A credit card allows customers to make purchases using an approved credit limit and repay the amount later.

7. What are banking charges?
Banking charges are fees collected by banks for certain services. These may include account maintenance, ATM usage, and transaction-related fees.

8. How can I open a savings account?
Customers can generally apply through a bank branch or the bank's official website, subject to identity verification and bank requirements.
